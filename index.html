Enter rest in an approved sleeper berth as rest. Rest in the sleeper berth of a stationary vehicle is stationary rest. Under Standard Hours, only the 24-hour rest can be taken in a sleeper berth while the vehicle is moving, and the NHVR recommends doing that only before the major rest break.

BFM accreditation granted before 1 August 2026 stays in force until it expires, is cancelled, or the operator moves to Heavy Vehicle Accreditation. New operators use Alternative Compliance Hours (ACH), which are set on each operator's certificate and are not checked here. Risk categories follow Schedule 2 of the regulation, including escalated risk for more than 15½ hours work in 24 hours.

How to enter time: each sheet starts as rest all day. Drag your finger or mouse along the top WORK row to change that time to work, and along the bottom REST row to change it back to rest. Each box is 15 minutes. The middle sheet is today. On a phone, scroll a sheet sideways by dragging the hour numbers.

Times are in the time zone of the driver's base. Periods are counted forward from the end of each relevant rest break; time before the first sheet counts as rest. Rest requirements are only checked for periods that end within the three sheets.

This is a guide only. Always check the current requirements with the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator (NHVR). AFM hours and Alternative Compliance Hours (ACH) are set on each operator's certificate and are not checked here.
