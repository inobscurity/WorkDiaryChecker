    Worked out as if you work without a break from the start time, then rest. Your entries before the start time are kept. A break of the length shown resets that limit. Plan your rest before you reach it, and stop sooner if you feel tired.

    How to enter time: each sheet starts as rest all day. Drag your finger or mouse along the top WORK row to change that time to work, and along the bottom REST row to change it back to rest. Choose Rest, not stationary above the sheets to draw rest in a seat or, for two-up drivers, in a moving sleeper berth: it shows as a dashed line and doesn't count as stationary rest. Each box is 15 minutes. The middle sheet is today unless you change its date, and the orange line marks the time now. Tap a red BREACH bar to see which rule was broken and the period it covers. On a phone, scroll a sheet sideways by dragging the hour numbers.

    Enter rest in an approved sleeper berth as rest. Rest in the sleeper berth of a stationary vehicle is stationary rest. Under Standard Hours, only the 24-hour rest can be taken in a sleeper berth while the vehicle is moving, and the NHVR recommends doing that only before the major rest break.

    BFM accreditation granted before 1 August 2026 stays in force until it expires, is cancelled, or the operator moves to Heavy Vehicle Accreditation. New operators use Alternative Compliance Hours (ACH), which are set on each operator's certificate and are not checked here.     Times are in the time zone of the driver's base. Time is counted as on the NHVR "Counting time" page: periods under 24 hours from the end of any rest break, and longer periods from the end of the major rest break that period requires (for example 7 hours for 24 hours, a night rest break for 14 days), or from the end of any rest break if that break hasn't been taken. A later major rest break inside a period does not end it. Time before the first sheet counts as rest, and rest requirements are only checked for periods that end within the three sheets. Totals entered for work before the sheets are checked against the 7, 14 or 28 days ending with the middle sheet.

    This is a guide only. Always check the current requirements with the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator (NHVR). AFM hours and Alternative Compliance Hours (ACH) are set on each operator's certificate and are not checked here.
